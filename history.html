History

Paperboy History Timeline
Year Event Platform Developer / Publisher Significance
The Beginning of Paperboy
1985 Original Release Arcade Atari Paperboy makes its arcade debut and introduces players to its newspaper-delivery gameplay.
1986 Home Computer Expansion Commodore 64, ZX Spectrum and others Atari / Elite Systems Paperboy expands beyond arcades through versions created for home computers.
1988 NES Release Nintendo Entertainment System Atari / Mindscape Paperboy reaches Nintendo players through a home-console version of the arcade game.
1990 Portable Release Game Boy Atari / Mindscape Paperboy becomes portable with its release on Nintendo's Game Boy.
The Paperboy Series Expands
1991 Paperboy 2 Multiple Platforms Tengen / Mindscape Paperboy 2 continues the series and appears on several home gaming systems.
2007 Digital Re-release Xbox 360 Midway The original Paperboy reaches a newer generation through a digital console release.
